Settings

Features

Only on your own copy. Mine turns a feature on or off for you. GitHub decides whether everyone who installs Sothis from GitHub gets it (their Sothis checks every few hours).

FeatureMineGitHub
GitHub token
Needed for the GitHub switches. Make a fine-grained token on github.com (Settings → Developer settings) with Contents: Read and write on your Sothis repo, and paste it here. It's stored encrypted on this PC only.

Window

Daily start screen
The first time you open Sothis each day: the weather, what's out today and what you did yesterday.
Accent color
The color of buttons, switches and highlights.
Logo
Use your own PNG everywhere the Sothis logo shows: the top bar, the big background crest, the start-up screen, the taskbar and the tray.
Background
How dark Sothis's black background is. Lower lets more of your desktop show through.

Always on top
Keep Sothis above your other apps.
Open at startup
Start Sothis when you sign in to Windows.
Mini player
Shrink to a small bar with the music controls.

Backup

Back up your settings
Save your settings, friends, pinned games and notes to a file. Passwords, secrets and sign-ins are never included.
Restore a backup
Load a backup file. Your current passwords and sign-ins stay as they are.

Discord

Shows your current voice call on Home. Uses your own Discord application from discord.com/developers. Add http://localhost as a redirect under OAuth2.

Show what I'm doing on Discord
Your Discord status shows the song you're playing and the game you're in, with your rank (Siege, CS2, Overwatch) and the map when Sothis knows it, including your Switch.
Idle status
When no song or game is on, your Discord status shows "Idle on PC".
Mute and deafen hotkeys
Mute or deafen in Discord from anywhere, even inside a game. A small pop-up shows which way it went. Click a key to change it.
Crunchyroll in your status
Shows the show, season and episode (like S1 E4) and the episode's time bar while you watch. Needs the small Sothis extension in the browser you watch in.
Just say "Sothis"
The member list shows "Playing Sothis" instead of the song or game. The details still show when someone opens your profile.
Spot the map in Overwatch and Siege
While one of them is in front, Sothis glances at your screen once a minute and puts the map in your Discord status. Needs Borderless or Windowed mode.
Voice overlay
Shows who's in your call on top of your games, with a green glow on whoever is talking. Clicks go straight through it.
Only show who's talking
Keeps the overlay out of the way until someone speaks.

Gaming

Games Sothis recognises
Sothis spots games from your Steam and Epic libraries, launcher folders and a public list of over 10,000 game programs. If it misses one, open the game, come back here and mark it. Wrongly picked up a program? Mark it as not a game.
Match found alert
When your game finds a match while you're tabbed out, Sothis plays a chime and shows a banner with a button that takes you back to the game.
Lower the music while someone talks
Turns Sothis down while someone else speaks in your Discord call, then back up.
Lower the music while a game is in front
Keeps the music quieter whenever you're in a game.

Clips

Sothis records the last moments of your game on your graphics card while you play, ready to save as a clip.

Record clips while you play
Starts when a game opens and stops when it closes. Nothing is kept unless you save a clip.
Clip key
Press it in game to save a clip. The Clip it button on Home does the same.
Clip length
How much of what just happened goes in each clip.
Record my mic
Adds your microphone to clips, on top of game and chat sound.
Game sound
How loud your game, music and chat are in clips.
100%
Discord voice
Your friends in your Discord call, kept on their own track so you can turn them down later in the editor.
100%
Mic
How loud your microphone is in clips.
100%
Mic noise suppression
Removes keyboard clicks, fans and background noise from your mic, like Medal does. Game sound isn't touched.
Sound timing
If the sound in your clips is early or late, nudge it here.
Hide Sothis from clips
Sothis's windows and pop-ups don't show up in your clips while it's recording. Windows hides them from Discord screen share on that screen too, so turn this off if you share Sothis on the screen your game is on.
Only the game window
Clips show just your game, not the rest of your screen, when it's not full screen.
Quality
High looks like Medal's default. Max is sharper still with bigger files. Clips are saved straight from the recording, without a second compression.
Frame rate
60 is smoother, 30 makes smaller files.
Screen
Which monitor to record. Auto uses the one your mouse is on when the game starts.
